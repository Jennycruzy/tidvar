How a tab works

Two transactions, any number of payments.

Your walletone shielded payment
The taba lock that needs both keys
The servicepaid every 10 s, off-chain
  1. 1OpenYour device and the service make a joint key neither can spend alone. The service also seals its half to a future time, your refund time, and your device checks that seal before you pay.
  2. 2UseEvery payment is a signed running total: a note that lets the service take this much. It costs milliseconds and nothing goes on chain.
  3. 3CloseOne transaction pays the service what you used and returns the rest. If the service vanishes, the seal opens at the refund time and you take everything back alone.

Who sees what

One tab, seen from every side.

The same tab of 400 payments, from the point of view of everyone who touches it. Nothing below is a promise: each line follows from what that party's own software receives.

Never sees

You

Never need

  • An account, an email or a card
  • To trust the service with your money

Why a tab

A minute of VPN is worth 10 zat. One Zcash payment costs 10,000 zat in fees.

Paying per minute or per call with ordinary transactions costs more in fees than the thing itself. A tab pays the fee twice, once to open and once to close, however many payments happen in between.
