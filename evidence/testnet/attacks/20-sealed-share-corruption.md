A service that seals bad key shares is caught. With one opened share corrupt, the payer refuses before funding ("The service's sealed key failed our check, so we didn't open the tab. No money moved."). With the unopened half corrupt, releasing the refund key fails cleanly. With exactly 32 of 64 shares valid, 0 of 10,000 random challenges passed; the exact chance is 1 in C(64,32), about 5.5 × 10⁻¹⁹. Tests: `cargo test -p tidvar-seal --test attacks`.
