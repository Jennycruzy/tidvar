The first mainnet tab after NU7 activated (block 3,543,405). The owner opened a 0.0005 ZEC, 2-hour tab on the public mainnet page and paid it from a Zingo wallet; both transactions are version 6 and carry the NU7 consensus branch (77190ad9). The payer's page did not notice the confirmed payment for about 20 minutes. The same kind of failure was confirmed in the service's saved view of another tab, whose last block had been replaced in a reorg, so no later block linked to it. Reloading the page rebuilt its view and found the payment at once, but the tab's closing time had passed: the service closed alone with the newest signed amount, 600 zat (one hour of VPN paid ahead), and returned 39,400 zat to the payer's Zodl address. Both the page and the service now rebuild a saved view of the chain that stops linking to it.
