The owner opened a 0.0005 ZEC, 30-minute tab on the public mainnet page from an Android phone and paid it from a Zingo wallet. The phone connected the mainnet WireGuard VPN and paid off-chain as it went (7 zat by the time the service packed its close for the watchtower). The watchtower, which holds no keys, then published that finished close by itself: the service received 7 zat and 39,993 zat returned to the payer's Zodl address. This run exposed two problems, both fixed after it: the watchtower acted before the service's own closing time (b12bdc7), and the VPN firewall dropped tunnel traffic to the service's own address (46928d8).
